**Key insight:** The meta-problem constrains everything. The methodology was PROCESS; articulation typically delivers CONTENT.

### INVERT (Failure Modes)

1. Describe the journey instead of creating it
2. Lead with the topic (triggers apparatus)
3. Write for already-converted or unreachable
4. Academic framing (separates what must be combined)
5. Overclaim for the LLM
6. Separate AI findings from political stakes
7. Ignore form (form IS content)
8. Too long / too short

**Central tension:** Process vs Content. The methodology worked as experience; can it transfer as description?

## Assessment of Exploration Structure

### What Worked

- Systems map captured problem space
- Inversion generated specific failure modes
- Perspectives forced challenging viewpoints
- Edges produced creative options
- Meta caught the distancing pattern

### What Didn't Work

- Output was predictable - phases ran but didn't surprise
- Generated analysis, not experience
- Skeptic perspective was strawman not steelman
- Didn't produce the shift the corpus says is necessary

### Implication for /deepthink Design

The structure produces good analytical output but may enable distancing. The META phase caught this, which is what META is for. But catching ≠ correcting.

**Open question:** Can an exploration structure produce insight, or only organize it? Does the structure itself prevent the intimacy the corpus identifies as mechanism?
